I take an AI idea from the meeting where someone first says it out loud to a product the business runs on and pays for. Eight years, more than fifty production systems, and the same pattern every time. Find the real problem underneath the brief. Design the system. Lead the team that builds it. Stay until the numbers move.

The domain is always new. The ability to figure it out is not.

## What I do now

I am Principal Machine Learning Engineer at [Zazmic](https://zazmic.ai), a Google Cloud Premier Partner. I lead and mentor the ML engineering team building multi-agent systems on Google ADK, the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform, Gemini and Claude. Our clients are in media, healthcare, finance and retail supply chains. I work alongside Google as a partner and directly with client teams at Apple and Meta.

I am still the most hands-on person on the team. I write the architecture, review the code and debug the agents. On the same day I run the presales call, the client delivery call and the management call. I care as much about evaluation, guardrails and cost per task as I do about the model. An agent nobody trusts is a demo, not a product.
