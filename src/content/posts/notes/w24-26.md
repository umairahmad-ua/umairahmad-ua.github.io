## Tuesday

Anthropic released [Claude Fable 5 and Claude Mythos 5](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-fable-5-mythos-5). Fable is the generally available model in a new tier above Opus. Mythos is the same model without some of the safety measures, for approved organizations only. This is the public Mythos-class release that Glasswing promised two weeks ago.

I had Fable 5 running against the operations agent eval set by Tuesday evening. Forty incident scenarios. It matched Opus 4.8 on every one and gave shorter remediation proposals. Shorter is good. The on-call engineer reads these at two in the morning. Then I ran it on the migration validation task, where it has to read a table of column match rates and decide whether to recommend cutover. It caught the timezone shift case from May on the first pass, without the per-column check we added. That got my attention. It does not mean I remove the check. It means the check and the model now agree, and I like two independent signals.

## Wednesday

[Code with Claude Tokyo](https://claude.com/code-with-claude) happened. I did not follow it closely. The release on Tuesday was the news.

## Friday

The US government [applied export controls](https://www.anthropic.com/news/redeploying-fable-5) to Fable 5 and Mythos 5. Anthropic restricted access for users outside the United States. My team is distributed across four countries. By Friday afternoon three of my the engineers could not call the model that the rest of us had spent the week evaluating.

I will not comment on the policy. I will comment on what it does to an engineering plan. A model is now a dependency with a jurisdiction. Our clients' agents run in Google Cloud regions their compliance teams chose. The people who build those agents sit wherever they sit. Until Friday those were separate questions. I spent the afternoon making sure every agent in production has a fallback model in its config, and that the fallback is tested. Most already did. Two did not. They do now.

Fable 5 is the best model I have used. For the moment, it is also the one I can least plan around.
