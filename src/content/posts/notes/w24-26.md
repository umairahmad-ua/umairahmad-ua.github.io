## Tuesday

Anthropic released [Claude Fable 5 and Claude Mythos 5](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-fable-5-mythos-5). Fable is the generally available model in a new tier above Opus. Mythos is the same model without some of the safety measures, for approved organizations only. This is the public Mythos-class release that Glasswing promised two weeks ago.

I had Fable 5 running against the operations agent eval set by Tuesday evening. Forty incident scenarios. It matched Opus 4.8 on every one and gave shorter remediation proposals. Shorter is good. The on-call engineer reads these at two in the morning. Then I ran it on the migration validation task, where it has to read a table of column match rates and decide whether to recommend cutover. It caught the timezone shift case from May on the first pass, without the per-column check we added. That got my attention. It does not mean I remove the check. It means the check and the model now agree, and I like two independent signals.
