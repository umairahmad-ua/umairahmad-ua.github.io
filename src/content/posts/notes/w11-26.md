Thursday was a big day for the kind of work I do.

## The Claude Partner Network

Anthropic held its first Partner Summit and launched the [Claude Partner Network](https://thenextweb.com/news/anthropic-commits-100m-to-claude-partner-network) with a $100M commitment. Alongside it they announced a [Claude Certified Architect – Foundations](https://www.channeldive.com/news/anthropic-launches-claude-channel-partner-program/814569/) exam.

I have built on Claude for client work for over a year without any formal credential. Google has certifications and a partner tier. Zazmic is a Google Cloud Premier Partner and that label opens doors on presales calls. Anthropic had nothing equivalent until this week. I registered for the exam the day it was announced. I plan to sit it before the end of the month and write about what it actually tests.

## A2A 1.0

The [A2A protocol reached 1.0](https://a2a-protocol.org/latest/blog/2026/03/12/a2a-protocol-ships-v10-production-ready-standard-for-agent-to-agent-communication/) the same day, with signed Agent Cards and governance under the Linux Foundation. MCP solved how an agent talks to a tool. A2A is for how an agent talks to another agent across a trust boundary.
