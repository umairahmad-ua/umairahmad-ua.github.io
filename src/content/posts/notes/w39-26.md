Tuesday was the day. Anthropic released [Claude Opus 5.5](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-opus-5-5) at four dollars in and twenty out, 40 percent below Opus 5. OpenAI answered within hours with [GPT-6 Sol and Luna](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-sol-and-luna/) at half the GPT-5.6 price. Monday had already brought [Grok 4.7](https://x.ai/news/grok-4-7) at two and six.

## What happened to the spreadsheet

I keep a cost model per production agent. Each step has a model, an average token count and a price. On Wednesday I re-ran it. The migration validator, which uses the most expensive model for the longest reasoning, dropped by about a third per batch. The ops agent barely moved, because most of its cost is tool calls and human review time, not tokens.

That second finding is the one I care about. When model price halves and your cost per task falls ten percent, the model was never the cost. I wrote the full breakdown as a deep dive this week.

## The harness is free now

AWS [open-sourced the Strands harness](https://strandsagents.com/blog/introducing-strands-harness/) on Monday. An agent harness is the loop around the model, the part that decides when to call a tool and when to stop. A year ago every team wrote its own. Now there are several good ones under permissive licenses. The part of my job that was harness engineering is shrinking. The part that is tool design and evaluation is not.

## The marketplace

Anthropic launched the [Claude Marketplace](https://claude.com/blog/claude-marketplace) on Wednesday with connectors, plugins and service partners in one place. We are a service partner through Zazmic. The interesting question for me is which of our internal MCP servers belong in a public directory and which stay with the client. That is next week's writing.

## Elsewhere

Google confirmed [Gemini 4 is in post-training](https://9to5google.com/2026/09/24/google-says-gemini-4-release-is-coming-as-soon-as-possible/) and made [Flash TTS generally available](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/changelog). Microsoft [relaunched Copilot](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2026/09/25/introducing-the-new-copilot-with-home-code-and-autopilot/) with persistent agents that carry an Entra ID identity, which is the permission model I have argued for all year. And the [DC Circuit upheld](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/25/pentagon-anthropic-ai-risk-appeals-court.html) the Pentagon designation of Anthropic. None of our clients are defense. Two of them asked anyway.
