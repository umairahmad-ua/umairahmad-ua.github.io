Tuesday was the day. Anthropic released [Claude Opus 5.5](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-opus-5-5) at four dollars in and twenty out, 40 percent below Opus 5. OpenAI answered within hours with [GPT-6 Sol and Luna](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-sol-and-luna/) at half the GPT-5.6 price. Monday had already brought [Grok 4.7](https://x.ai/news/grok-4-7) at two and six.

## What happened to the spreadsheet

I keep a cost model per production agent. Each step has a model, an average token count and a price. On Wednesday I re-ran it. The migration validator, which uses the most expensive model for the longest reasoning, dropped by about a third per batch. The ops agent barely moved, because most of its cost is tool calls and human review time, not tokens.
