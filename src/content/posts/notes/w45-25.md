## Kimi K2 Thinking

Moonshot released [Kimi K2 Thinking](https://simonwillison.net/2025/Nov/6/kimi-k2-thinking/) on Thursday. It is an open reasoning model that scores near the frontier labs on agentic benchmarks and can run hundreds of tool calls in sequence. I pulled it into our eval harness on Friday.

On the Scout research-assistant task it did well. Better than I expected on planning, slightly worse on following our output schema. Those two results together tell me something. The reasoning is there. The instruction-following polish is what the closed labs still sell. For an internal tool I would consider it. For a client-facing agent that has to produce a fixed report format, not this month.
