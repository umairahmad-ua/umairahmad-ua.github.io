Two platform announcements in one week, from the two vendors I spend most of my time with.

## What happened

- OpenAI held DevDay and released [AgentKit](https://openai.com/index/introducing-agentkit/), a visual agent builder, plus an Apps SDK. The Apps SDK is built on MCP. OpenAI adopting Anthropic's protocol for its own app platform is the most important line in the whole event.
- Google released the [Gemini 2.5 Computer Use](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/gemini-computer-use-model/) model in preview. It clicks and types in a browser. Useful for legacy systems with no API, which describes half of enterprise software.
- Google Cloud launched [Gemini Enterprise](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/introducing-gemini-enterprise), the successor to Agentspace. It is the front door for agents inside a company, with connectors to the systems people already use.

## What I make of it

MCP won the interface war this week. When OpenAI builds its app platform on a protocol Anthropic designed, the argument about which tool format to use is over. Our tool contracts on the Scout agents are already MCP-shaped. That decision looked cautious in July. It looks obvious now.

Gemini Enterprise is what my clients will actually see. Most of them do not want to build agents. They want to use one that knows their documents and their CRM. As a Google Cloud partner, this is the product I will be asked to implement most in the coming year. I read the docs twice this week and started a checklist of what connectors our media and finance clients would need.

Visual agent builders like AgentKit worry me a little. They make the demo fast. They make the eval and the rollback slow. I have watched this pattern before with low-code ETL tools. The drag-and-drop version is never the one that runs the business.
