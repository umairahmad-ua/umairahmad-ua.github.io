I watched [Google Cloud Next](https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/google-cloud-next/google-cloud-next-2026-wrap-up) from Houston this year, Wednesday through Friday, with half the team on the same stream. As a Premier Partner we had briefings ahead of time. The public version still surprised me in places.

## The platform gets a name

The [Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform](https://www.hpcwire.com/aiwire/2026/04/23/google-unveils-gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/) is generally available. It is the evolution of Vertex AI, with Agent Engine, ADK, and the Gemini Enterprise app under one roof, plus an agents-cli. Our Scout agents at Let's Forage have run on Agent Engine since last autumn. Nothing in our deployment broke this week. The names changed and the console moved. What changed underneath is the story Google is telling clients. A year ago I explained Vertex AI as a place to train and serve models. Now the pitch is a place to run agents, and models are a line item inside it. That is how our clients already think. The product caught up with the sales conversation.

The agents-cli is the piece I will use most. Deploying an ADK agent to Agent Engine has been a Python script and a prayer. A first-party CLI with a diff of what is about to change is what I asked our Google contacts for in December.

## Meanwhile, two frontier models

OpenAI released [GPT-5.5](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/23/openai-chatgpt-gpt-5-5-ai-model-superapp/) on Thursday and DeepSeek put out [V4-Pro and V4-Flash](https://www.deeplearning.ai/the-batch/kimi-k2-6-matches-open-qwen3-6-max-anddeepseek-v4-falls-just-behind-top-closed-models) with a million tokens of context on Friday. In any other week either would lead this note. I noted both and went back to the Next sessions. That is not a judgment on the models. It is a statement about where my time is best spent. A better model helps every agent by a few percent. A better platform changes what my team can maintain.

## What I make of it

Google's agent story is coherent now. I have been telling clients that for a year and it was partly faith. This week it became something I can point at.
