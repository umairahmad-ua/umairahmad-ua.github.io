The last full working week of the year and nobody slowed down.

## Gemini 3 Flash

Google released [Gemini 3 Flash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gemini_(language_model)) on Wednesday. This matters more to me than the Pro release did in November. Most of the agents I run for clients do not need the biggest model. They need a model that is fast, cheap, and predictable across thousands of calls a day. The Scout agents for Let's Forage make dozens of sub-calls per session. Every one of those calls is a Flash candidate. I have a swap test queued for January.

## Agent Skills as an open standard

Anthropic published [Agent Skills as an open standard](https://siliconangle.com/2025/12/18/anthropic-makes-agent-skills-open-standard/) on Thursday. A skill is a folder with instructions and optional scripts that an agent loads when the task matches. I have been building the same idea by hand for months. We call them playbooks. The format was ours, and every client got a slightly different version. An open format removes that mess. I am less interested in the marketing and more in the fact that a Claude skill and a Gemini skill could share a file layout.

## GPT-5.2-Codex

OpenAI released [GPT-5.2-Codex](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5-2-codex/) the same day. The coding model cadence is now roughly monthly. I do not switch coding models monthly. My team settles on one for a quarter and learns its habits. The gains from knowing a model well beat the gains from a benchmark bump.

## What I make of it

Two of these three releases are about packaging, not intelligence. Skills are packaging. Flash is a price point. That is where the real work is for people like me. The frontier keeps moving, but the enterprise problems are about cost, repeatability, and who owns the instructions. This week was a good week for all three.
