## Gemini 3

Google released [Gemini 3 Pro](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/gemini/gemini-3-collection/) on Tuesday. It went into Search, the Gemini CLI, and Vertex AI on day one. For a team building on Google Cloud, that last part is what matters. No waiting list. I had it in our staging environment by Wednesday morning.

Results on our evals. The Scout data-analysis agent improved on multi-step reasoning over BigQuery results. The persona agent, which is mostly writing, was roughly unchanged. The migration agent's SQL translation got better on window functions, which is where it had been weakest. I am moving the orchestrator and the translation agent to Gemini 3 after one more week of shadow traffic. The writing agents stay where they are. There is no reason to pay more for the same output.

## Antigravity

Google also released [Antigravity](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/gemini/gemini-3-collection/), an agent-first IDE. Every lab now has one. Cursor, Claude Code, Codex, Antigravity. My team uses two of them and argues about it weekly. I do not mind. The argument keeps everyone honest about what the tools actually do.

## The Microsoft triangle

[Microsoft, NVIDIA and Anthropic](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2025/11/18/microsoft-nvidia-and-anthropic-announce-strategic-partnerships/) announced a three-way partnership. Anthropic commits 30 billion dollars to Azure. NVIDIA and Microsoft invest in Anthropic. Claude is now on all three major clouds with real capacity behind it. For a client who wants model choice without cloud lock-in, that is the best news of the year.
