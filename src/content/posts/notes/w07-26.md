## Two open models

Z.ai released [GLM-5](https://huggingface.co/blog/mlabonne/glm-5) on Wednesday, a 744B mixture of experts. MiniMax followed with [M2.5](https://www.minimax.io/news/minimax-m25) on Thursday. That makes four large open-weight releases from Chinese labs since late January.

I get asked about open models on almost every client call now. The question is usually framed as cost. My answer is usually about control. An open model lets you pin a version forever. A hosted model changes under you. For the migration track, I want the translation agent to behave the same way in batch twelve as it did in batch one. That argument for open weights is stronger than the price argument.
