## Glasswing widens

Anthropic [expanded Project Glasswing](https://www.anthropic.com/news/expanding-project-glasswing) on Tuesday to critical infrastructure operators in more than fifteen countries. They also said Mythos-class models will reach the public. I read the post twice for the sentence about timing. It says soon and does not say when.

One of our clients is a healthcare payer. Their security team asked me on Wednesday whether the Glasswing findings apply to the systems we built for them. The honest answer was that I do not know, and that the model is not available to us to check. That is a strange position for a vendor. I can tell them the architecture is sound. I cannot tell them a model that finds flaws better than any tool I have used has looked at it. I expect that conversation to repeat with every regulated client until the public release.

## Build, briefly

[Microsoft Build](https://news.microsoft.com/build-2026-live-blog/microsoft-build-2026-live/) ran Tuesday and Wednesday. Agent 365 and Microsoft IQ were the headlines. Our clients are on Google Cloud, so I read the coverage the way I read GTC in March. It tells me what enterprise buyers will ask for in six months, because Microsoft sells to the same procurement teams we do. The ask this time is agent governance as a product. Who can deploy an agent, what it can touch, and where the log lives. We answer those questions in a document today. Soon we will need to answer them in a console.
