This is the first of these notes. I have been at Zazmic for three months now, leading the engineers on agent systems for enterprise clients. Every week something changes in the tooling. I keep losing track of what mattered and what was noise. So I am writing it down, once a week, in a few hundred words.

## What happened

OpenAI released [GPT-5-Codex](https://openai.com/index/introducing-upgrades-to-codex/) on Monday. It is a version of GPT-5 tuned for long-running coding work. The interesting part is not the benchmark. It is that OpenAI now treats "agent that runs for hours" as a product category with its own model.
