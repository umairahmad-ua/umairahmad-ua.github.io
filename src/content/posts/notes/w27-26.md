Tuesday was the busiest release day since April. Three things landed that touch our stack directly.

## Sonnet 5

[Claude Sonnet 5](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-sonnet-5) has a million tokens of native context. Not a beta flag. Native. I ran the migration validation agent against it on Wednesday. That agent reconciles a translated stored procedure against the original and the sample data. With Opus 4.8 we chunked the larger procedures and stitched results. With Sonnet 5 the whole procedure, the target SQL and the reconciliation report fit in one call.

Fewer calls did not mean better answers by default. The model reads more but it still skips. We kept the chunked path as a second opinion for anything over 40K tokens. Trust is earned per task, not per model.

## Fable 5 is back

The export controls on Fable 5 [were lifted on June 30](https://www.anthropic.com/news/redeploying-fable-5) and the model returned worldwide on July 1. Eighteen days of restriction. My engineers in Pakistan and Ukraine lost nothing because we never pinned the operations agent to Fable. I wrote about that last week. This week confirmed it.
