## Three clouds, three agent runtimes

[Amazon Bedrock AgentCore](https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2025/10/amazon-bedrock-agentcore-available) reached general availability on Monday. That makes three managed agent runtimes I now have to understand. Vertex AI Agent Engine on Google Cloud, AgentCore on AWS, and whatever OpenAI is assembling out of AgentKit. They all solve the same problems. Sessions, memory, tool gateways, identity, observability.
