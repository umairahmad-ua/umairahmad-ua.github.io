Monday was busy. Anthropic released [Claude Sonnet 4.5](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-sonnet-4-5) and Claude Code 2.0 on the same day. Sonnet 4.5 is pitched as the best model for long agentic tasks. I ran it against our Scout eval set on Tuesday. It was better on multi-step tool use. It was not better on short extraction. The eval told me that in an hour. The launch post could not.
