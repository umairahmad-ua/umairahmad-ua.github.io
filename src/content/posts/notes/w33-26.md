## Thursday

[Auto mode became the default](https://claude.com/blog/auto-mode-default-in-claude-code) in Claude Code on Thursday for Pro, Max and Team plans. Last week I said I would have waited a quarter. I watched what happened on my team instead of guessing.

Nothing broke. The whole team, four days, zero incidents. Two of them had never turned auto mode on and did not notice the change until Friday standup. That tells me the permission prompts were already noise for experienced users. It also tells me nothing about a first-week user. My team is not the population I was worried about.
