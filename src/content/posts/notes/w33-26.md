## Thursday

[Auto mode became the default](https://claude.com/blog/auto-mode-default-in-claude-code) in Claude Code on Thursday for Pro, Max and Team plans. Last week I said I would have waited a quarter. I watched what happened on my team instead of guessing.

Nothing broke. The whole team, four days, zero incidents. Two of them had never turned auto mode on and did not notice the change until Friday standup. That tells me the permission prompts were already noise for experienced users. It also tells me nothing about a first-week user. My team is not the population I was worried about.

I am keeping my opinion and lowering my confidence in it. That is what evidence is for.

## A billion

Google said [Gemini passed one billion monthly active users](https://blog.mean.ceo/new-ai-model-releases-news-august-2026/) on Tuesday. I do not build for consumers. But I do build on Gemini for clients whose customers use Gemini on their phones. The model my agents run on is the model their users already talk to. That changes expectations in a way no benchmark does. A Scout user in a marketing team expects the assistant to behave like the one in their pocket.

## DeepSeek V4-Pro and Gemini 3.7 Flash

DeepSeek made [V4-Pro official](https://www.evertune.ai/resources/ai-model-tracker) on Wednesday after the April debut. Google released [Gemini 3.7 Flash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gemini_(language_model)) on Thursday. Two Flash releases in four weeks. I moved the research assistant agent to 3.6 Flash three weeks ago. I am not moving it again until the eval set says so. Chasing point releases is how you turn a stable agent into a science project.

## What I told the team

Model releases are weather. The tool contracts, the eval set and the approval flow are the building. We check the weather. We do not rebuild the house for it. That was Friday's standup in one sentence. The rest was about a BigQuery reconciliation that was off by eleven rows, which is the actual job.
