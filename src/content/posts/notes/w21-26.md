## ADK 2.0 is real now

[Google I/O](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/google-io-2026-collection/) ran Tuesday and Wednesday. ADK Python 2.0 went GA. Gemini 3.5 Flash went GA. Antigravity got a 2.0 with a desktop app and a CLI. For my team the ADK release is the one that changes the calendar.

The engineer I assigned to the 2.0 preview in March gave her report. Graph workflows handle the critic-to-author loop we wanted for Scout. The trace in Agent Engine shows each edge as a span, which is the thing I said would decide it. So we are moving. Not all nine agents at once. The data analysis pair first, because it has the best eval coverage and the smallest blast radius if the loop misbehaves. Then the campaign author. Then the rest over the summer. The client sees no change unless we get it wrong.

## The name goes away

On Thursday Google [retired the Vertex AI name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gemini_Enterprise_Agent_Platform). Everything is the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform now. I have "Vertex AI" on my resume, in three client proposals, and in about forty internal documents. The console redirects. The docs redirect. The habit will take a year. I went through the proposals on Friday and left the old name in one of them on purpose, because the client's procurement team approved that document and I am not going to reopen it over a rebrand.

## MCP tightens up

The MCP project published a [release candidate](https://blog.modelcontextprotocol.io/posts/2026-07-28-release-candidate/) for its next spec. A stateless core, extensions instead of a growing base, and harder OAuth rules. I wrote about tool contracts in December and argued that most of the pain is in state. A stateless core says the spec authors reached the same view. Our Gemini Enterprise connectors are MCP servers into CRMs and BigQuery. I will read the RC closely before the final lands in July. If the auth changes are what they look like, the connectors need a revision.

Three announcements, one direction. The platform is getting more opinionated. I like that. Opinions I can plan around. Options I have to test.
