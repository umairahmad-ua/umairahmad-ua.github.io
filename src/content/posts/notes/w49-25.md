## The open models had a good week

Mistral released the [Mistral 3 family](https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-3/). Large 3 is a 675 billion parameter mixture of experts under Apache 2.0. The small Ministral models come in 14, 8, and 3 billion sizes. DeepSeek finalized [V3.2](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/news/news251201/) with a Speciale variant for reasoning.

Apache 2.0 at that size changes a conversation I have with regulated clients. Healthcare and finance teams ask whether they can run a model inside their own boundary. Until recently the honest answer was "yes, but you give up a lot." The gap is smaller now. I fine-tuned Llama and Mistral models with QLoRA at Developers Inc for exactly these cases. A stronger open base means less fine-tuning to reach the bar. I am scheduling an eval run against our claims-style extraction tasks for next week.

## re:Invent

[AWS re:Invent](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/aws/aws-re-invent-2025-ai-news-updates) brought Nova 2 models, Trainium3, and previews of AgentCore Policy and AgentCore Evaluations. I do not build on AWS much these days. I read the evaluations announcement closely anyway. Every cloud is converging on the same idea. An agent platform needs a built-in way to score agent runs, and the cloud wants to own that. I am fine with that as long as I can export the scores. Our eval harness writes to BigQuery. If a platform wants to consume that, good. If it wants to replace it, no.

## Deep Think

Google rolled out [Gemini 3 Deep Think](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/gemini/gemini-3-deep-think/) to Ultra subscribers. Extended reasoning modes are useful for the planning step of an agent and wasteful everywhere else. I want a knob per agent, not per account.

The week's real work was boring. We wrote forty more eval cases for the supply-chain planning agents. Planners at the apparel client reviewed them. Eleven were wrong. We fixed them. That is the job.
