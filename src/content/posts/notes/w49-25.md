## The open models had a good week

Mistral released the [Mistral 3 family](https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-3/). Large 3 is a 675 billion parameter mixture of experts under Apache 2.0. The small Ministral models come in 14, 8, and 3 billion sizes. DeepSeek finalized [V3.2](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/news/news251201/) with a Speciale variant for reasoning.

Apache 2.0 at that size changes a conversation I have with regulated clients. Healthcare and finance teams ask whether they can run a model inside their own boundary. Until recently the honest answer was "yes, but you give up a lot." The gap is smaller now. I fine-tuned Llama and Mistral models with QLoRA at Developers Inc for exactly these cases. A stronger open base means less fine-tuning to reach the bar. I am scheduling an eval run against our claims-style extraction tasks for next week.
