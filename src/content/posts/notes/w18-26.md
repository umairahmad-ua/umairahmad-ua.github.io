## The routing eval, finally

I promised results from putting small models into the Scout routing slot. Here they are, with the caveat that this is one client's traffic and one month of held-out sessions.

The routing task is simple to describe. A user message arrives at the root agent. The agent picks one of eight specialists or answers directly. We score exact match against a human label. Gemini Flash is the incumbent and it is what runs in production today.

Mistral Small 4 matched Flash within the noise on English marketing queries. It fell behind on messages that mixed a brand name with a data question, the case where the right answer is the sequential analysis pair rather than the campaign author. Qwen3.6-35B-A3B was a point or two behind Small 4 and faster. Neither beat Flash. Neither was far off.
