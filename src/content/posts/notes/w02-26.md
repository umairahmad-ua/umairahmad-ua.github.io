The year opened with hardware and healthcare.

## What happened

- NVIDIA used its [CES keynote](https://www.axios.com/2026/01/05/nvidia-ces-2026-jensen-huang-speech-ai) on Monday to show the Vera Rubin platform. More compute, on schedule.
- OpenAI launched [ChatGPT Health](https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-health/) on Wednesday, a consumer product for medical questions with record connections.
- Anthropic launched [Claude for Healthcare](https://fortune.com/2026/01/11/anthropic-unveils-claude-for-healthcare-and-expands-life-science-features-partners-with-healthex-to-let-users-connect-medical-records/) today at the JPM conference, aimed at providers and life sciences.
