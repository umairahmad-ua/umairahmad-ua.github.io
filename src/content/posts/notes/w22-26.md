## What happened

- Anthropic published a [Project Glasswing update](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/05/26/anthropic-project-glasswing-update/) on Tuesday. The restricted Mythos model has found more than ten thousand software flaws with its security partners.
- [Claude Opus 4.8](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/whats-new/2026-w22) came out Thursday. An incremental step over 4.7. Our cloud operations agent moved to it on Friday after the eval suite passed.

## What I make of it

Ten thousand is the number I keep turning over. At Qwiet AI I worked on Ocular, which found data-flow vulnerabilities by scoring paths on a code property graph. We measured success in findings per repository and in how many of those a human confirmed. A good week was dozens of confirmed findings across a customer base. A general model at ten thousand in a few weeks is not the same activity. It is closer to a census.

The part of Autofix that I designed was the loop after the finding. One agent proposed a patch. A second checked it was semantically correct. A third ran the regression suite. Only then did a human see the fix. Finding flaws was never the bottleneck once the models got good. Fixing them without breaking the build was. If Glasswing partners are now sitting on ten thousand findings, the interesting engineering is happening in that loop, and nobody is publishing about it yet.
