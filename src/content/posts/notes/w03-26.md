Monday brought two announcements that will shape the year. Tuesday brought one that changed my week.

## Gemini in Siri

Apple and Google signed a [multi-year deal](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/12/googles-gemini-to-power-apples-ai-features-like-siri/) for Gemini to power the next Siri. I work with Apple teams through the Let's Forage platform, and I build on Gemini every day at Zazmic. This is the first time those two facts point in the same direction. It also settles a question I get on presales calls. Clients ask if betting on Gemini is safe. Apple just answered that for me.

## Claude Cowork

Anthropic put out [Claude Cowork](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/01/claude-cowork/) as a research preview on macOS. It is an agent that works across files and apps on your machine. I tried it for two afternoons. It is early. What interests me is the shape, not the polish. A desktop agent with file access is the same architecture I build for clients, pointed at a laptop instead of a data warehouse. The permission model is the whole product. Anthropic seems to know that.

## Veo 3.1 for the media clients

Google updated [Veo 3.1](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/ai/veo-3-1-ingredients-to-video/) with 4K, vertical output, and Ingredients to Video. Vertical output is the one that matters. The Scout agents for Let's Forage analyze short-form video from TikTok and Instagram. Until now, generation and analysis lived in different aspect ratios. That gap is closing. I spent Wednesday scoping what a generation agent would look like next to the analysis agents. Not building yet. Scoping.

## What I make of it

Distribution is becoming the story. Gemini gets Siri. Claude gets the desktop. The models are close enough that where they run decides who wins. For someone building on GCP for enterprise clients, this was a good week.
