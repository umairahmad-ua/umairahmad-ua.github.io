## Computer use is generally available

Anthropic made [computer use, the browser-use tool, the Skills API and the Files API generally available](https://github.com/jqueryscript/anthropic-claude-timeline) on the Claude Platform, and launched Claude Academy the same day.

I have avoided computer use in client work for a year. Not because it did not work. Because a GA label is what a client's risk team needs before an agent is allowed to click on their systems. Now it has one.

The first place I will use it is not glamorous. The apparel client has a supplier portal with no API. A planner logs in every Monday and downloads four spreadsheets. That is the whole integration. A browser-use tool with a narrow allowlist and a screenshot audit trail replaces that. It will not replace the planner. It replaces the part of her Monday she hates.

## ADK TypeScript 2.0

Google released [ADK TypeScript 2.0](https://adk.dev/2.0/) on Friday. Python went GA in May. Go in June. TypeScript now. The set is complete. Our client platform teams can pick the language they already run and get the same agent model and the same evaluation hooks. I argued for ADK over LangGraph a year ago partly on this bet. The bet paid.

## Two smaller items

Claude Code opened a [/design research preview](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/whats-new/2026-w34). It drafts a UI from a description. My team will try it on internal tools and nowhere else for now.

[Claude Cowork reached web and mobile](https://github.com/jqueryscript/anthropic-claude-timeline) for all paid plans on Tuesday. Cowork is the file-and-task agent that started as a macOS preview in January. I do not use it for engineering work. Our clients' operations teams might.

## The pattern

Every capability I turned down in 2025 for lack of a GA label has one now. Computer use, browser use, skills, files. The reasons to say no are running out. The reasons to say "narrowly, with an audit trail" are not. That is the sentence I will be saying for the next year.
