Thanksgiving week in the US, so a short one. Two releases, both from Anthropic's side of the world.

## Opus 4.5

[Claude Opus 4.5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claude_(language_model)) came out on Monday. A week after Gemini 3 Pro. The two frontier models are now close enough that the choice for most of our tasks comes down to price, latency, and which cloud the client already pays. That is a good place for the industry to be. Two years ago there was one obvious model. Now there is a market.

I ran Opus 4.5 on the Autofix-style verification task I care most about. Given a proposed code change and a test suite, decide whether the change is safe. It was careful. It flagged two edge cases the previous version missed. Careful is exactly what I want from a verifier. Speed matters less there.

## MCP at one year

The [MCP specification dated 2025-11-25](https://blog.modelcontextprotocol.io/posts/2025-11-25-first-mcp-anniversary/) landed on the protocol's first birthday. Three changes matter to me.

First, tasks. Long-running operations now have a defined lifecycle, so a tool can say "this will take a while, poll here." Our migration agent needed that. Translating and validating a thousand stored procedures does not fit in one request. Second, OIDC discovery for authentication, which removes a pile of custom glue on the enterprise side. Third, URL-mode elicitation, so a server can send the user to a web page to approve something. That is how approval gates should work.

A year ago MCP was a spec from one vendor. Now OpenAI builds on it, Google supports it, and it has a versioned spec with a real changelog. I told the team in July to shape our tool contracts around it. I am glad we did.

Quiet week at work. The reconciliation checks on the migration agent held through a full test cutover. No incidents.
