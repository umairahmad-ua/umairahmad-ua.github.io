Thanksgiving week in the US, so a short one. Two releases, both from Anthropic's side of the world.

## Opus 4.5

[Claude Opus 4.5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claude_(language_model)) came out on Monday. A week after Gemini 3 Pro. The two frontier models are now close enough that the choice for most of our tasks comes down to price, latency, and which cloud the client already pays. That is a good place for the industry to be. Two years ago there was one obvious model. Now there is a market.

I ran Opus 4.5 on the Autofix-style verification task I care most about. Given a proposed code change and a test suite, decide whether the change is safe. It was careful. It flagged two edge cases the previous version missed. Careful is exactly what I want from a verifier. Speed matters less there.
