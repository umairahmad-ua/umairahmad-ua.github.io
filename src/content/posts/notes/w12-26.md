I sat the Claude Certified Architect Foundations exam on Tuesday and passed. Anthropic opened the certification at its [Partner Summit](https://www.channeldive.com/news/anthropic-launches-claude-channel-partner-program/814569/) the week before, and I wanted to see what they think an architect should know. Short answer: less about prompts than I expected, more about tool contracts, context limits, and failure handling. That matches what breaks in our systems. The exam is scenario-based. You get a client situation and pick the design that holds up. I would have failed it two years ago and not because of the model knowledge.
