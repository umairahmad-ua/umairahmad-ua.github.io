The first week of September had two frontier releases in three days. I will start with the one that changed a number on my dashboard.

## Fable 5.1 and the cache

Anthropic released [Claude Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1](https://venturebeat.com/technology/anthropics-claude-fable-5-1-and-mythos-5-1-arrive-with-a-75-cost-reduction-for-fable-cache-reads) on Tuesday. The headline for me is not the model. It is cache reads at a quarter of the old price.

Our operations agent carries a long system prompt. The playbooks, the permission model, the schema of every tool. That prefix is identical on every call and it is cached. Cache reads were 30 percent of the agent's monthly bill. As of Wednesday they are closer to 8. Same agent, same traffic, a different invoice. I have argued for a year that cost per task decides whether an agent survives its first budget review. This week the vendor moved that number for me.

The model itself is better on our eval set. Not by much. The cache is the story.

## GPT-6 Astra

OpenAI released [GPT-6 Astra](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/03/open-ai-astra-gpt-6-cyber.html) on Thursday, GA on Friday. It has computer use built in and it is the first OpenAI model to cross their critical cyber threshold. That second fact will matter more than the first. A model that a lab classifies as critical for cyber capability is a model a bank's risk team will ask about by name. I have no eval numbers yet. I have a meeting on Tuesday where I will be asked what Astra means for a migration agent that reads stored procedures. My answer is that the risk is in what the agent is allowed to do, not in which model reads the code. That was true before Thursday.

## Flash and Spark

Google released [Gemini 3.8 Flash and Meta released Muse Spark 1.3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gemini_(language_model)) on Wednesday. Third Flash release since July. I am holding the research assistant on 3.6 until the quarter closes.

## The quiet number

Three frontier releases this week and the one that mattered was a pricing footnote. If you run agents for a living, read the pricing page before the model card. The model card tells you what is possible. The pricing page tells you what you will still be running in six months.
